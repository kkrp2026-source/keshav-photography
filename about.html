About Us

Keshav

Founder & Visionary

The Artist Behind the Lens

Keshav

With over 16 years of passionate dedication to the art of photography, Keshav has established himself as one of the most sought-after wedding photographers in South India. His journey began with a simple camera and an extraordinary vision to capture life's most precious moments.

Based in Eluru, Andhra Pradesh, Keshav Photography has expanded its reach to Hyderabad, across India, and internationally to the USA and Dubai. Every frame we capture is a testament to our commitment to excellence, creativity, and the timeless art of visual storytelling.

Our philosophy is simple: every couple has a unique love story, and we believe in telling it through photographs that evoke emotion, capture authenticity, and create lasting memories that transcend time.
